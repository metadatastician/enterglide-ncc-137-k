== Intent (recorded 2026-09-02, owner ruling)

This investigation starts handling the topology of knots: that is what the K at the end of the code refers to. NCC is a reference to the galaxy and space catalogue acronyms of astronomy (with the Star Trek registry echo intended), and 137 points at the "Varying Constants" hypothesis: the idea that the equivalence around the number 137, the inverse fine-structure constant, may change over time, so that is the focus. How knots are represented on the field, and what gates they must pass, is not yet specified.
